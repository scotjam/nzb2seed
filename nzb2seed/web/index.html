Build a torrent from Usenet

  1. Search for a release or a season.
  2. Pick the torrent on the left; the matching NZBs on the right are ticked for you.
  3. SABnzbd downloads the best-matching post: +Repair when the torrent holds RARs, +Repair/Unpack when it holds unpacked files, never +Delete.
  4. The files are arranged exactly like the torrent: Sample, Proof and Subs folders, text files only fixed when that makes their pieces verify, everything else removed.
  5. qBittorrent gets the torrent stopped, pointed at the files, and rechecked.
  6. You end with 100.0% and nothing downloaded over BitTorrent.
